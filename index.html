Asset Setup Guide

Har item apne aap <category>/pic/<item-slug>.jpg aur <category>/3d/<item-slug>.glb dhundta hai (index.html jis main folder me hai, usi ke andar har category ka apna folder).

Bas usi naam/path par apni photo (.jpg/.png) aur 3D model (.glb/.gltf) daal dijiye — image na milne par card automatically ek placeholder icon dikhata hai, aur model na milne par 3D popup me "3D model jaldi aa raha hai" dikhta hai.

Chahe to MENU_DATA me kisi bhi item ke andar img: ya model: field daal kar custom path bhi de sakte hain.
